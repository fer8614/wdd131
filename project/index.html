Season & Safety

  • Late spring often brings the strongest flows after snowmelt.
  • Stay on marked trails—mossy basalt and cliff edges are slippery year-round.
  • Carry rain layers; gorge and Cascade weather can shift within an hour.
  • Check road and trailhead alerts before remote Umpqua or McKenzie trips.
