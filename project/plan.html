Before You Go

  • Footwear: Waterproof boots with good tread handle spray zones and mud.
  • Layers: Temperatures drop near canyon floors even on sunny afternoons.
  • Passes: Many Forest Service trailheads need a Northwest Forest Pass or day fee.
  • Pets: Confirm dog rules before you drive—some gorge trails prohibit pets.
  • Timing: Arrive early for Multnomah, Tumalo, and other popular weekend lots.
  • Safety: Never climb past railings for photos; falls and basalt are unforgiving.
