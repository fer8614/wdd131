W05 Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Oregon Waterfalls Guide · oregonwaterfallguide.com

This name clearly describes the site’s focus: a practical guide to Oregon’s waterfalls. It is easy to remember, search-friendly, and matches the project subject. The optional domain reinforces the brand for locals and visitors planning outdoor trips.

2. Site Purpose

Oregon Waterfalls Guide is an interactive online resource that helps hikers, families, and travelers discover waterfalls across Oregon and plan safe, enjoyable visits.

The site will provide:

  • An organized directory of notable Oregon waterfalls with trail details, difficulty, distance, pet policies, and parking notes.
  • Region-based browsing and filters so users can find falls by area or trip needs.
  • Photo galleries, seasonal timing tips, and safety guidance for each location.
  • A Hidden Gems section highlighting lesser-known waterfalls for adventurous hikers.
  • A contact / trip-planning form so visitors can request tips or share feedback (planned for Week 06).

Planned pages for Week 06: Home, Waterfalls Directory, and Plan a Visit (form + tips), with consistent navigation and a shared visual theme.

3. Scenarios

These questions represent the target audience—Oregon locals and tourists planning outdoor trips—and will guide the site’s content.

  • Which waterfall near Portland has an easy trail that is dog-friendly and has reliable parking?
  • What is the best season to visit Multnomah Falls, and what safety tips should I know before I go?
  • Where can I find lesser-known “hidden gem” waterfalls if I want a quieter hike away from crowded viewpoints?

4. Color Schema

The palette draws from Oregon forests, cascading water, and misty trail mornings. These colors are applied throughout this planning document.

  • Forest Green #1B4332

    Header, footer, primary headings, and strong brand surfaces.

  • Cascade Teal #0E7490

    Links, accents, section borders, buttons, and navigation highlights.

  • Moss #52796F

    Secondary accents, wireframe outlines, and supporting UI details.

  • Mist #EEF5F2

    Page background and soft content panels.

  • Charcoal #1F2933

    Body paragraph text for readable contrast on light backgrounds.

5. Typography

Two Google Fonts keep the site readable and distinctive: an editorial serif for titles and a clean sans-serif for body content.

Heading font

Fraunces

Used for page titles and section headings (h1–h3).

Body font

Source Sans 3 — Trail notes, difficulty ratings, and visitor tips stay clear and comfortable to read on phones and desktops.

Used for paragraphs, lists, navigation labels, and form text.
